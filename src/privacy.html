Privacy Policy – BLNK Studio

At BLNK Studio, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the data you share with us through our website, advertising campaigns, and lead forms.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with us (for example, by filling out a lead form, subscribing to updates, or contacting us), we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Organization/College/Business details
  • Marketing and admissions-related requirements
  • Website or social media links you provide
  • Education and coaching institutes (spoken English, NEET, UPSC prep)

We do not collect sensitive personal information unless voluntarily provided.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Contact you regarding our services
  • Assess your business needs and provide tailored solutions
  • Share insights, offers, or updates relevant to your business
  • Improve our marketing campaigns and service delivery

We will never sell or rent your data to third parties.

3. Data Sharing

We may share your information only with:

  • Internal BLNK Studio team members
  • Trusted partners or service providers who assist us in delivering our services (bound by confidentiality agreements)
  • Authorities, if required by law

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@theblnkstudio.com

6. Third-Party Links

Our website or ads may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us: hello@theblnkstudio.com
